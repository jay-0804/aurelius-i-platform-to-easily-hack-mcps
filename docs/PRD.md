# Product Requirements Document (PRD): EasyMCP
=====================================================

## Vision
--------

EasyMCP is a platform designed to simplify the process of hacking Maximum Credit Points (MCPs) for small companies. Our vision is to provide a user-friendly and efficient solution that enables small businesses to easily manage and optimize their MCPs, saving them time and resources.

## Problem
--------

Small companies often struggle to manage their MCPs due to limited resources and lack of expertise. The current process is manual, time-consuming, and prone to errors, resulting in:

* Inefficient use of resources
* Missed opportunities for optimization
* Increased risk of non-compliance

## Solution
---------

EasyMCP is a web-based platform that provides a simple and intuitive way for small companies to hack their MCPs. The platform will offer the following key features:

* Automated MCP calculation and optimization
* Real-time tracking and monitoring
* Customizable alerts and notifications
* Integration with existing accounting and ERP systems

## Target Users
-------------

The primary target users for EasyMCP are:

* Small business owners and managers
* Accounting and finance professionals
* Operations managers

These users will benefit from EasyMCP's ease of use, automation, and real-time tracking capabilities, allowing them to focus on core business activities.

## MVP Features
--------------

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for EasyMCP will include the following features:

1. **MCP Calculator**: A simple and intuitive calculator that allows users to input relevant data and receive an optimized MCP calculation.
2. **Real-time Tracking**: A dashboard that displays real-time MCP data, including current balances, usage, and alerts.
3. **Customizable Alerts**: Users can set up custom alerts and notifications to inform them of MCP-related events, such as low balances or optimization opportunities.
4. **Integration with Accounting Systems**: EasyMCP will integrate with popular accounting and ERP systems to streamline data exchange and reduce manual entry.

## Metrics
--------

The following metrics will be used to measure the success of EasyMCP:

1. **User Acquisition**: Number of new users acquired per month
2. **User Retention**: Percentage of users retained over a 6-month period
3. **MCP Optimization Rate**: Average percentage of MCP optimization achieved by users
4. **Customer Satisfaction**: Measured through surveys and feedback forms

## Roadmap
---------

The development roadmap for EasyMCP is as follows:

* **Month 1-3**: Research and development of the MVP features
* **Month 4-6**: Alpha and beta testing with a small group of users
* **Month 7-9**: Launch and marketing of the MVP
* **Month 10-12**: Gather feedback and iterate on the product

## User Stories
--------------

The following user stories outline the key requirements for EasyMCP:

1. **As a small business owner, I want to be able to easily calculate my MCPs so that I can optimize my usage and reduce waste.**
2. **As an accounting professional, I want to be able to track my clients' MCPs in real-time so that I can provide them with accurate and timely advice.**
3. **As an operations manager, I want to receive customizable alerts and notifications so that I can stay on top of MCP-related events and make informed decisions.**
4. **As a user, I want to be able to integrate EasyMCP with my existing accounting and ERP systems so that I can streamline my workflow and reduce manual entry.**

By addressing these user stories and delivering a simple, intuitive, and automated solution, EasyMCP aims to become the go-to platform for small companies looking to hack their MCPs.
